Methodology

For each employee, historical compensation is compounded forward using annual CPI for that employee's country. Every salary is expressed at the current year's price level where CPI data is available. The highest inflation-adjusted salary becomes the strongest historical compensation benchmark. The latest recorded salary is adjusted to the current year and compared with that benchmark to calculate the raise required for equal purchasing power. The status also checks whether the latest compensation record belongs to the current calendar year.

Prototype CPI dataset covers North Macedonia, Uruguay, Argentina, Brazil, Netherlands and United States for 2017–2026. 2025–2026 figures may include estimates and should be replaced with finalized official observations when available.
